How this was counted

Every message was laid out and every attachment converted just as a full run would do it, and the pages were counted. The index at the front of Volume 01 was laid out too. Notes that stand in for an attachment are counted as one page each, which is what they are. A full run with the same settings should come within a few pages of this total. The START HERE page is not counted: it is a separate file.
